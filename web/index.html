Your data & the source

When you submit, postal codes and parcel details go through CoolCode’s gateway to Canada Post. Your target date stays in this browser. Plans are saved only if you choose Save; device storage is shared with others using this browser. Exported comparisons include your entered postal codes. Opening a map sends the office coordinates to OpenStreetMap. No names, street addresses, customer accounts or payment details are requested.

Live source: Canada Post Rating and Post Office Services. Only services returned as eligible are shown; commercial account discounts are not used.
